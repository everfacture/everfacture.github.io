TB Target 2030: A Pipe Dream?

Indonesia, second globally for TB cases with 1.09 million estimated infections and 134,000 annual deaths, faces a serious challenge to its 2030 elimination target. Dr. Ari Baskoro from Unair's Faculty of Medicine questions the feasibility, citing the disease's complexity beyond simple contagion. This isn't just a health issue; it flags potential strain on public health infrastructure and workforce productivity, impacting the broader economic outlook.

BPKH Funds Post-Eid Commute

The Hajj Fund Management Agency (BPKH) sent 675 Surabaya workers back to Jakarta and surrounding areas after Eid holidays, using 15 buses. This "Balik Kerja Bareng BPKH" program, funded by the *Dana Abadi Umat* (Endowment Fund for the Ummah), not hajj deposits, aims for safe and affordable travel. It's a free service, including basic provisions, for those returning to work, with 1,412 applicants for 675 spots in Surabaya alone. Nationally, 2,700 participants were accommodated from four cities.
