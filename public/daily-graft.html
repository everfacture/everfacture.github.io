Indonesia Startup Funding Resets Amid Global Headwinds

Indonesia's startup ecosystem saw USD 355.7 million in funding across 91 deals in 2025, a significant drop from 2021's USD 6.9 billion peak, signaling a shift towards selective growth. New Retail led with USD 130.9 million, followed by Fintech and E-Commerce. Early-stage deals dominated in volume, but Series A-C rounds captured 80% of capital, with debt funding reaching USD 264 million. M&A surpassed IPOs as the primary exit strategy, reflecting market consolidation.
